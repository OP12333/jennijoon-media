JenniJoon Uploader

A private publishing tool used only by the creator Jenni Joon.

JenniJoon Uploader is a small desktop script that uploads Jenni Joon's own finished short videos to her own YouTube channel (Jenni Joon) and her own Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Signs in once with the channel owner's Google account (OAuth).
  • Checks that the signed-in channel is "Jenni Joon" before every upload.
  • Uploads a video file from the creator's computer with title and description.

It does not read, analyse or store data from other YouTube users.
